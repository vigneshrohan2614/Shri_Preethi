My girl...

I have thought about how to write this so many times. Every time I start I realise there are just too many things I feel for you. Somehow the simplest truth is still the one that feels the most honest.

I like you. I really like you. More than I expected. More than I planned. More than I know how to explain without sounding like my heart has completely lost its mind over you.

And it isn't just because you are beautiful. Of course I notice your beauty. I notice your eyes. Your eyelashes. Your hair. Those little bangs that fall over your face and somehow make you look even cuter. I notice your smile. I notice your soft little palms. I notice the tiny things that other people might miss.

But the truth is, I fell for something much deeper than all of that.

I fell for the person you are.

Your heart. Your character. Your kindness. The way you care. The way you support me. The way my happiness genuinely matters to you. The way you want to see me succeed and feel proud when something good happens to me.

I love your voice too. There is something about hearing you that can make an ordinary day feel softer. Sometimes I don't even need a big conversation. I just like having you there.

I love the way you understand my goofy side. You don't make me feel silly for being silly. You actually love seeing that part of me. You love seeing the little boy inside me happy and that means more to me than you probably realise.

With you I don't feel like I always have to be the strong one. I can be playful. I can be clingy. I can be completely ridiculous. I can let my guard down.

And when you care for me so gently, when you want to caress me, when you call me Daddy in that cute way of yours, I swear there is a part of me that just melts.

You make me feel wanted. You make me feel cared for. You make me feel like I can be completely myself and still be loved.

That is something I don't take lightly.

How am I supposed to not fall for this smile? ♡
The part I don't say enough

You became special quietly...

I don't think there was one dramatic moment when everything changed. It happened in little pieces.

A conversation here. A caring message there. The way you check on me. The way you listen. The way you want to know if I am okay. The way you celebrate the things that make me happy.

And somewhere in between all those little moments, you became someone I started looking forward to.

Someone whose messages I want to see.

Someone whose voice I want to hear.

Someone whose happiness I genuinely care about.

Someone I want to protect. Someone I want to support. Someone I want to annoy forever with my stupid jokes.

Someone I want to make smile on the days when she doesn't feel like smiling.

That someone is you, Preethi.

I love that we can like each other without having to explain every little feeling. Sometimes our hearts seem to understand things before our words do.

Maybe that is why being around you feels so natural to me. There isn't a performance. There isn't a need to pretend. It just feels like two people who found something they genuinely like in each other.

And I want to take care of that feeling.

I want to give it time. I want to give it memories. I want to give it silly moments and beautiful ones too.

I want to know the girl behind every smile. I want to know your dreams. I want to hear about the things you are excited about. I want to be there when you achieve them.

Because I don't just want to admire you. I want to know you completely.
